    Tutorial: Take Screenshots#

    Learn to capture professional screenshots of your Unreal Engine work.

    What You'll Learn#

    • Basic screenshot capture
    • Camera positioning
    • High-resolution captures
    • Composition techniques

    Prerequisites#

    • A scene with actors, materials, and lighting
    • Completed previous tutorials (or any interesting scene)

    Step 1: Basic Screenshot#

    The simplest capture:

    "Take a screenshot"

    This captures the current viewport exactly as you see it.

    Default Location

    Screenshots save to [Project]/Saved/Screenshots/

    Step 2: Position the Camera#

    Get a better angle:

    "Move the camera to 0, -500, 200 looking at the origin"

    Then capture:

    "Take a screenshot"

    Step 3: Frame Your Subject#

    Focus on a specific object:

    "Frame the cube in the viewport"

    Or look at a specific point:

    "Point the camera at position 100, 0, 50"

    Step 4: Adjust Field of View#

    Change the lens:

    "Set viewport FOV to 60"
    FOV Effect Use Case
    30-50 Narrow, compressed Portraits
    60-75 Normal General
    90-120 Wide, dramatic Environments

    Step 5: High Resolution#

    Capture at higher quality:

    "Take a 4K screenshot"

    Or specify exact resolution:

    "Take a screenshot at 3840x2160"

    Step 6: Named Screenshots#

    Organize your captures:

    "Take a screenshot named 'FinalScene_Front'"
"Take a screenshot named 'FinalScene_Top'"

    Step 7: Multiple Angles#

    Capture a turntable:

    "Take screenshots from 8 angles around the center object, 45 degrees apart"

    Or specific views:

    "Take screenshot from front view, name it 'Front'"
"Take screenshot from left view, name it 'Left'"
"Take screenshot from top view, name it 'Top'"
"Take screenshot from a 45-degree angle, name it 'Hero'"

    Step 8: Clean Captures#

    Remove UI elements:

    "Take a screenshot without UI"
"Capture viewport only, hide all overlays"

    Composition Tips#

    Rule of Thirds#

    "Position camera so the main subject is at the right third of the frame"

    Leading Lines#

    "Frame the shot to use the floor lines leading to the subject"

    Depth#

    "Add foreground elements for depth"
"Enable depth of field, focus on the subject"

    Quality Settings#

    Enable High Quality#

    "Set viewport quality to cinematic"
"Enable ray tracing for screenshot"

    Post-Processing#

    "Add slight bloom for the screenshot"
"Enable color grading"

    Capture Workflow#

    Product Shot#

    "Product shot workflow:
1. Position object at center
2. Set up three-point lighting
3. Use neutral background
4. Camera at 45-degree angle
5. FOV 50 for minimal distortion
6. Take 4K screenshot"

    Environment Shot#

    "Environment shot workflow:
1. Find interesting composition
2. Use wide FOV (90)
3. Low camera angle for drama
4. Ensure good lighting coverage
5. Take screenshot at high res"

    Batch Captures#

    All Cameras#

    "Take a screenshot from each camera actor in the level"

    Documentation Set#

    "Create documentation screenshots:
- Wide establishing shot
- Detail shots of key features
- Top-down layout view
- Each named appropriately"

    Challenge: Photo Session#

    Create a professional photo session of a simple scene:

    1. Set up a scene with 3 objects
    2. Add proper lighting
    3. Take 5 different shots:
    4. Hero shot (main angle)
    5. Close-up detail
    6. Wide environment
    7. Top-down
    8. Dramatic low angle
    Solution 
    "Photo session:

Scene setup:
- Spawn sphere at 0,0,100
- Apply gold metallic material
- Three-point lighting

Shot 1 - Hero:
- Camera at 300,-300,200 looking at 0,0,100
- FOV 60
- Take screenshot named 'Hero'

Shot 2 - Close-up:
- Camera at 100,-100,120 looking at 0,0,100
- FOV 40
- Take screenshot named 'CloseUp'

Shot 3 - Wide:
- Camera at 0,-800,300 looking at 0,0,100
- FOV 90
- Take screenshot named 'Wide'

Shot 4 - Top:
- Camera at 0,0,500 looking straight down
- Take screenshot named 'TopDown'

Shot 5 - Dramatic:
- Camera at 200,-200,50 looking up at 0,0,100
- FOV 70
- Take screenshot named 'Dramatic'"

    Troubleshooting#

    Issue Solution
    Dark screenshot Check lighting, increase intensity
    Blurry Disable motion blur, check focus
    Wrong size Specify exact resolution
    UI visible Use "without UI" option
