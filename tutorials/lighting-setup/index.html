    Tutorial: Lighting Setup#

    Learn to illuminate your scenes with professional lighting techniques.

    What You'll Learn#

    • Adding different light types
    • Three-point lighting setup
    • Light properties and colors
    • Creating mood with lighting

    Prerequisites#

    • Some actors in your level
    • Materials applied (optional but recommended)

    Step 1: Add Your First Light#

    Start with a simple point light:

    "Add a point light at 0, 0, 300"

    This adds an omnidirectional light above the origin.

    Step 2: Adjust Light Properties#

    Make it brighter and warmer:

    "Set the point light intensity to 5000"
"Set the light color to warm white (3000 Kelvin)"

    Step 3: Add a Spotlight#

    Create focused lighting:

    "Add a spotlight at 500, 0, 400 pointing at the origin"

    Adjust the cone:

    "Set the spotlight outer cone angle to 45 degrees"
"Set intensity to 8000"

    Step 4: Three-Point Lighting#

    The classic setup for showcasing objects:

    Key Light (Main Light)#

    "Add a spotlight called 'KeyLight' at 500, -300, 400"
"Point it at 0, 0, 100"
"Set intensity to 10000"
"Set color temperature to 5500K"

    Fill Light (Softer, Opposite Side)#

    "Add a point light called 'FillLight' at -400, 200, 300"
"Set intensity to 3000"
"Set color temperature to 6000K"

    Back Light (Rim/Separation)#

    "Add a spotlight called 'BackLight' at 0, 500, 350"
"Point it at 0, 0, 100"
"Set intensity to 6000"
"Set outer cone to 30 degrees"

    Step 5: Ambient Light#

    Add overall illumination with a sky light:

    "Add a sky light"
"Set sky light intensity to 1.0"

    This fills in the shadows.

    Step 6: Directional Light (Sunlight)#

    For outdoor scenes:

    "Add a directional light for sunlight"
"Rotate it to point down at 45 degrees"
"Set intensity to 10 lux"
"Set color to daylight (5500K)"

    Step 7: Colored Lighting#

    Create mood with colored lights:

    "Add a point light at 300, 0, 200 with blue color"
"Add a point light at -300, 0, 200 with orange color"

    Color Temperature Reference#

    Temperature Color Mood
    2700K Warm orange Cozy, intimate
    3200K Warm white Indoor residential
    4000K Neutral Office, commercial
    5500K Daylight Natural outdoor
    6500K Cool blue Bright, clinical

    Step 8: Light Attenuation#

    Control how far light reaches:

    "Set the point light attenuation radius to 500"

    Smaller radius = more focused light.

    Step 9: Shadows#

    Enable and configure shadows:

    "Enable shadows on the key light"
"Set shadow resolution to high"

    For soft shadows: 

    "Set source radius to 50 for soft shadows"

    Step 10: PBL Presets#

    Instead of placing lights manually, you can apply a physically validated PBL preset in one step. PBL presets spawn a complete lighting rig with scientifically accurate lux values.

    Quick Start#

    List what's available: 

    "List all PBL lighting presets"

    Apply a preset: 

    "Apply the golden hour clear PBL preset"

    This spawns a DirectionalLight, SkyLight, SkyAtmosphere, ExponentialHeightFog, VolumetricCloud, PostProcessVolume, and CineCameraActor — all configured with validated physical values.

    Adjust After Applying#

    Fine-tune exposure without redoing the whole setup: 

    "Set PBL exposure to EV 10"

    When to Use PBL

    Use PBL presets when you want a fast, physically accurate starting point. You can always adjust individual lights afterward. For learning purposes, Steps 1-9 above give you more control over each piece.

    Practical Setups#

    Studio Lighting#

    "Create studio lighting setup:
- Key light (spotlight) front-right, high, pointing at center
- Fill light (point) front-left, medium height
- Back light (spotlight) behind, pointing at subject
- White floor for bounce"

    Dramatic Scene#

    "Create dramatic lighting:
- Single strong spotlight from above
- No fill light
- Deep shadows
- Slight blue ambient"

    Warm Interior#

    "Create cozy interior:
- Warm point lights (2700K) at ceiling positions
- Rect lights for windows (5500K)
- Low intensity sky light for ambient"

    Challenge: Light a Scene#

    Light this simple scene effectively:

    1. Floor plane
    2. Three objects (cube, sphere, cylinder)
    3. Create appealing lighting
    Solution 
    "Set up the scene:
- Spawn floor: plane at 0,0,0 scaled 10,10,1
- Spawn cube at -200,0,75
- Spawn sphere at 0,0,100
- Spawn cylinder at 200,0,75

Apply materials:
- Red to cube
- Gold metallic to sphere
- Blue to cylinder

Add lighting:
- Key: spotlight at 400,-400,500 pointing at 0,0,100, intensity 12000, 5000K
- Fill: point light at -300,300,300, intensity 4000, 5500K
- Back: spotlight at 0,600,400 pointing at 0,0,100, intensity 5000
- Ambient: sky light at intensity 0.5"

    Troubleshooting#

    Issue Solution
    Scene too dark Increase light intensity, add more lights
    Harsh shadows Increase source radius, add fill light
    Flat looking Add contrast, use key/fill ratio
    Colors look wrong Check color temperature, add color grading
